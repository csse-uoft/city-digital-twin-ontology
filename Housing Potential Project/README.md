# Housing Potential Project

This folder contains the historical Housing Potential project work, organized by the subject areas shown in the repository layout document.

## Structure

- **Buildings/** - building, ownership, federal-building, and height/zoning scripts.
- **Services and Amenities/** - childcare, community centre, fire, hospital, library, long-term care, parks, public schools, solid waste, and supermarket scripts.
- **Infrastructure/** - power feeder, sewer, and water scripts.
- **Transportation/** - transit route and transit stop scripts.
- **Road Network/** - ORN road-network implementation, mappings, diagrams, ontology references, supporting files, and the Housing Potential road script.
- **Parcels and Zoning/** - parcel and zoning scripts.
- **Capacity/** - capacity-focused scripts.
- **Housing Potential Diagrams/** - diagrams from the original Housing Potential work.
- **Housing Potential Documentation/** - documentation from the original Housing Potential work.

The former top-level `Housing Potential Python/` folder has been removed after its contents were reorganized here.
